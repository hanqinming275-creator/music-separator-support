MindTrace 隐私政策

更新：2026-10-18。开发者：Qinming Han。

笔记、笔迹、图片、文档、录音及转写结果保存在设备上。启用 iCloud 同步或云盘备份后，文件保存至你选择的服务，受其隐私政策约束。开发者没有接收笔记内容的服务器。

麦克风用于你主动开始的录音，相机和照片权限用于插入图片。可在系统设置管理权限。Apple 语音识别与翻译由 Apple 提供，处理方式取决于系统能力与服务设置；本地模型在设备上运行，下载模型会连接模型托管服务。

购买由 Apple 处理，应用读取购买权益以解锁功能，开发者不接收银行卡信息。应用不含广告和第三方广告追踪或分析 SDK，不出售个人信息。

可删除笔记、录音和模型并清空最近删除；云端副本需在对应服务管理。订阅可在 Apple 账户取消。

联系开发者：支持页面。请勿在公开问题中发布私人内容。

MindTrace Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026. Developer: Qinming Han.

Notes, handwriting, images, documents, recordings and transcripts are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync or cloud backups, files are saved with your selected provider under its privacy policy. The developer does not operate a server receiving your note content.

Microphone access is used for recordings you start. Camera and photo permissions let you insert images. Manage permissions in Settings. Apple speech recognition and translation are provided by Apple according to system capabilities and service settings. Local models run on your device; model downloads connect to hosting services.

Apple handles purchases. The app reads entitlements to unlock features. The developer does not receive payment card information. The app contains no advertising or third-party advertising tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

Delete notes, recordings and models in the app and empty Recently Deleted. Manage cloud copies with their providers. Cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account.

Contact the developer through the support page. Do not post private content in public issues.
