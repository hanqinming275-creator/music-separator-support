LiveTranslator Privacy Policy · macOS

生效及更新日期 / Effective and updated: 2026-10-06

本政策适用于 Qinming Han 提供的 LiveTranslator macOS 应用及其支持页面。联系方式：1708542206@qq.com。

This policy covers the LiveTranslator macOS app and its support page, provided by Qinming Han. Contact: 1708542206@qq.com.

1. 音频与屏幕权限 / Audio and screen permission

您主动开始翻译后，应用通过 Apple ScreenCaptureKit 获取系统播放音频，在本机进行识别和翻译。捕获范围可能包含多个 App 的声音，并非只限于某个选定 App。应用不提供麦克风输入模式。捕获接口可能产生低分辨率屏幕帧，应用立即丢弃这些帧，不显示、保存或上传它们；不录制视频文件。您可在 macOS 系统设置中管理屏幕与系统录音权限，点击“停止”结束捕获。

When you start translation, the app captures system playback audio using Apple ScreenCaptureKit and processes it on your Mac. Capture may include audio from multiple apps, rather than one selected app. There is no microphone input mode. The capture API may produce low-resolution screen frames; the app immediately discards them without displaying, saving, or uploading them and does not record video files. Manage recording permission in macOS System Settings and click Stop to end capture.

2. 本地识别与翻译 / On-device processing

当前版本使用 Apple SpeechAnalyzer / SpeechTranscriber 和 Apple Translation 的本地模型处理音频与文字，不将音频、识别原文或译文上传到开发者服务器，不接入第三方在线 AI 翻译服务。首次使用或模型更新可能需要从 Apple 下载语言资源；购买、恢复购买及系统资源下载需要网络。Apple 服务的数据处理适用 Apple 隐私政策。

The current version uses local Apple SpeechAnalyzer/SpeechTranscriber and Apple Translation models. It does not upload audio, transcripts, or translations to developer servers or use third-party online AI translation services. Language resources may download from Apple on first use or update. Purchases, restoration, and resource downloads need connectivity. Apple services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

3. 记录、导出与设置 / History, exports, and preferences

原文、译文及起止时间码保存在本次翻译会话的内存中，用于字幕和完整记录。关闭记录窗口不会清空记录；开始新会话会重置记录，退出应用后不保留会话历史。应用不自动保存原始音频。您主动导出 TXT / PDF 时，文字和可选时间码写入您选择的位置；如该位置属于 iCloud 或其他同步文件夹，文件可能由相应服务同步。您自行管理、分享或删除导出的文件。

Source text, translations, and start/end timecodes stay in session memory for captions and history. Closing the history window does not clear them; a new session resets them and quitting does not retain session history. The app does not automatically save raw audio. When you export TXT/PDF, text and optional timecodes are written to your chosen location. iCloud or other synced folders may sync files through their own services. You control exported files, including sharing and deletion.

界面语言、字幕大小等必要偏好设置保存在本机，不用于广告或用户画像。

Necessary preferences, such as interface language and caption size, are stored locally and are not used for advertising or profiling.

4. 购买与授权 / Purchases and access

订阅、Apple 官方免费试用、永久买断及恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用读取 Apple 验证的商品和交易信息（如商品 ID、交易状态、到期或撤销状态），仅用于判断授权及显示购买状态，不维护独立的本地试用计时。应用不会接收您的 Apple 账户密码、银行卡资料或支付凭据，也没有开发者自建账号系统。

Apple StoreKit handles subscriptions, Apple introductory trials, lifetime purchases, and restoration. The app uses verified product and transaction information, such as product IDs, transaction status, expiration, or revocation, only to determine access and display purchase status. There is no independent local trial timer. The app does not receive Apple account passwords, bank-card details, or payment credentials and has no developer-operated account system.

5. 日志与技术支持 / Diagnostics and support

应用可能在本机生成运行、耗时或错误诊断日志，不自动发送给开发者。显式启用的开发/支持诊断可包含识别文字、译文和时间信息。只有您主动提供邮件、截图、导出内容或日志时，开发者才会收到其中的信息，用于回复、排查和解决问题。请勿发送不必要的私人音频、文字或账户资料。支持邮件会经邮箱服务商处理；相关资料仅在处理问题及必要后续跟进期间保留，您可通过支持邮箱请求删除您提供的资料；依法必须保留的资料除外。

The app may generate local operational, timing, or error logs without automatically sending them to the developer. Explicitly enabled development/support diagnostics may include transcripts, translations, and timing. The developer receives information only when you choose to send an email, screenshot, export, or log, and uses it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send unnecessary private audio, text, or account details. Email is processed by email providers. Support materials are retained only for resolving the issue and necessary follow-up; request deletion through the support email, except where legal retention is required.

6. 不追踪、不出售 / No tracking or sale

当前应用未接入广告追踪、第三方分析 SDK 或开发者自动遥测服务。开发者不出售、出租或为广告目的共享您的音频、原文或译文。Apple、网站托管及邮件服务可能按照各自政策处理提供服务所需的数据。

The current app includes no advertising tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or automatic developer telemetry. The developer does not sell, rent, or share audio, transcripts, or translations for advertising. Apple, website hosting, and email services may process data needed to provide their services under their own policies.

7. 支持网站 / Support website

本支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，没有额外添加广告、分析脚本或追踪 Cookie。GitHub 会为安全目的记录访问者 IP 地址；其处理规则见 GitHub 隐私声明。不要把应用本地处理与网站托管日志混为一谈。

GitHub Pages hosts this site. We have added no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see its privacy statement. Website hosting logs are separate from the app’s local processing.

8. 您的选择与政策更新 / Your choices and changes

您可以停止捕获、撤销系统权限、删除导出文件，并请求删除主动发送的支持资料。功能或数据处理方式改变时，我们会更新本页面及日期。如有隐私疑问或请求，请联系 1708542206@qq.com。

You can stop capture, revoke system permissions, delete exports, and request deletion of support materials you sent. We will update this page and its date when features or data practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact 1708542206@qq.com.
