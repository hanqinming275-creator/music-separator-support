录屏助手隐私说明 / Screen Recorder Privacy

最后更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-09-12

1. 本地录制与文件

录屏助手在您的 Mac 上本地处理录制内容。视频、系统声音、麦克风声音与屏幕标注仅用于生成您主动开始的录制文件，并保存在您选择的文件夹中。应用不会将录制内容上传到开发者服务器。

Screen Recorder processes recordings locally on your Mac. Video, system audio, microphone audio, and annotations are used only to create recordings that you start and are saved in the folder you choose. The app does not upload recordings to developer servers.

2. macOS 权限

录制屏幕或系统声音需要 macOS 的“屏幕与系统录音”权限。只有在您开启麦克风录制时，应用才会请求麦克风权限。您可随时在 macOS 系统设置中更改这些权限。

Recording the screen or system audio requires macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission. The app requests microphone permission only when you enable microphone recording. You can change these permissions at any time in macOS System Settings.

3. 设置与试用状态

应用会在本机保存必要的偏好设置，例如界面语言、录制选项和最近选择的保存位置。若使用免费试用，试用状态也仅保存在本机，用于判断是否可以开始新的录制。

The app stores necessary preferences locally, such as interface language, recording options, and the most recently selected save location. If you use the free trial, its status is also stored locally only to determine whether a new recording can start.

4. 购买

永久解锁和恢复购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。应用不会接收或保存您的 Apple ID、付款信息或支付凭据。

Lifetime unlock purchases and Restore Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app does not receive or store your Apple ID, payment details, or payment credentials.

5. 数据收集与共享

录屏助手不提供开发者自建账号系统、广告追踪或开发者分析服务；不会出售、出租或向第三方共享您的录制内容。

Screen Recorder does not provide a developer-operated account system, advertising tracking, or developer analytics service. It does not sell, rent, or share your recordings with third parties.

6. 联系方式

如对技术支持或隐私说明有疑问，请发送邮件至 1708542206@qq.com。
